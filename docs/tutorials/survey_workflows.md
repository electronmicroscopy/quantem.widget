# Survey workflows

Use these workflows when you are working at the folder or session level instead
of opening one dataset at a time.

- [Folder survey](./survey): point `survey(folder)` at a microscopy folder to get a
  fast inventory, HAADF/STEM thumbnails, EDS launchers, and reversible curation
  state.
- [Field-of-view survey](./survey_fov): group repeated acquisitions of the same
  region, such as 0 degree, 90 degree, and EDS files, into compact FOV rows.

After selecting useful files, continue to [Saving and sharing](./widget_export) to
make notebooks or HTML files portable.
